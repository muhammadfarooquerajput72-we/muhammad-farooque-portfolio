# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are startup founders, small teams, and recruiters evaluating adaptable early-career technology talent. Secondary visitors include collaborators in AI video and YouTube automation, cybersecurity internship recruiters, IT support recruiters, and Virtual University students who relate to Muhammad's public learning journey.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio helps visitors understand Muhammad Farooque's developing capabilities, see honest evidence of practical work, and identify a suitable reason to contact him. Success means his range reads as a connected working method rather than a scattered collection of interests.

## Positioning

Muhammad works at the intersection of practical support, cybersecurity learning, AI-assisted software building, and AI-powered media. The portfolio makes that intersection legible through one progression: **Support → Secure → Build → Create**.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive from LinkedIn, a job application, a freelance conversation, or a shared project link. They need a fast first impression, then clear paths to work evidence, current learning, background, and contact information. The site is maintained as a statically exported Next.js application with versioned content owned by the project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four permanent pillars: Support, Secure, Build, and Create.
- Virtual University student-life content belongs to public learning, not a fifth professional niche.
- Cybersecurity is an active learning direction; authorized laboratory work must never be presented as professional penetration-testing experience.
- The site may show completed, in-progress, and planned projects, with evidence status explicit.
- The MVP has no authentication, database, CMS, analytics, payments, or server-side contact form.
- The public contact destination, CV download, GitHub URL, detailed YouTube attribution, and hosting platform remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Muhammad Farooque.
- Location: Islamabad.
- Core line: **Learning across tech. Building in public.**
- Voice: curious, practical, credible, open, and never inflated.
- Candidate B is the approved visual direction: warm technical editorial composition, connected orbital geometry, warm ivory, deep ink, orange, blue, and sage.
- Approved assets live at `public/brand/muhammad-farooque-profile.png` and `public/brand/candidate-b-banner.png`.
- The interface must not resemble a cyberpunk dashboard, generic AI landing page, résumé template, or collection of unrelated niche cards.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved square profile portrait and Candidate B banner.
- A supplied claim that a YouTube channel reached 50K+ subscribers; Muhammad's exact role and publishable proof still require confirmation before a detailed case study is presented as verified.
- A functioning local portfolio foundation with static routes, typed content contracts, component tests, browser tests, and recorded asset integrity.
- Current project evidence for the portfolio itself is represented in `src/content/projects.ts`.

No verified client names, testimonials, commercial results, cybersecurity findings, or revenue figures are available and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Connect the range through a visible working method.
2. Prove with process and evidence; do not inflate with titles.
3. Make exploration feel intentional and useful.
4. Let a visitor find the relevant doorway without hiding the whole person.
5. Keep public learning human, specific, and easy to update.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio targets WCAG 2.2 AA, remains fully usable by keyboard, preserves meaningful content without motion, respects reduced-motion preferences, and provides the same narrative and destinations on narrow and wide screens.
